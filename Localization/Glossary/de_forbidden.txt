# German forbidden vocabulary (owner rules of 2026-10-06: Docs/Design/Vocabulary_Rule.md, No_Sparkle_Rule.md).
# Nothing religious or divine, no sparkle words. Syntax (Drafts/check_drafts.py): a plain entry matches at the start
# of a word (stem, any ending); "=" the whole word only; "~" anywhere. Matching ignores case.
# Allowed look-alikes: Himmel (the sky only, as in Sternenhimmel), Kirsche (cherry), Segel (sail), Gebiet (area),
# gebeten (asked), Beton, Zentrum, Funk (radio), Wunsch (wish).
