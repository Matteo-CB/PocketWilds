# Pocket Wilds glossary: Spanish, Latin America (es-419)

Draft of 9 October 2026, agent TR2. Fixed terms for every text of the game in this language, so later texts stay
consistent. Source: `Localization/Drafts/es-419.json`. Forbidden words: `es-419_forbidden.txt`. Questions for the
native reviewer: `Drafts/NOTES_TR2.md`.

## Tone

- Neutral Latin American Spanish, « tú » (not « vos », not « vosotros »): warm, gentle, playful, never pushy.
- Goals are short tú imperatives: « Eclosiona 3 huevos », « Cepilla un animal ».
- Buttons are infinitives or short imperatives: Recoger, Recibir, Jugar, Continuar, Cerrar, Abrir, Cancelar.
- Gender-neutral greetings where Spanish would force a gender: « ¡Hola de nuevo! » (not bienvenido/a), « Bienvenidos al refugio ».
- Pan-Latin-American words: boletos, golosinas, moño, abrojos, ajolote, video, canasta, lindo.
- Numbers: decimal point as in CLDR es-419 (× 1.25); « 12% » without a space; units min, h, s with a no-break space.
- Opening marks ¡ ¿ always. No em dash.
- Brand: Pocket Wilds stays in Latin letters, never translated.

## Names in sentences

- `species.*.name_the` = el Conejo, la Ardilla, el Erizo; `biome.*.name_the` = la Pradera, el Bosque; zone names carry their article (la Pradera florida).
- Contractions T39B must handle: « a {animal} » (wish.015, 016, 019, 023, 035, 043, 050) and « a {visitor} », « a {destination} » become « al » before el; « de {animal} » (wish.047) becomes « del ».
- « para {animal} » and « en {biome_the} » never contract and are used where natural.
- Squirrel is feminine: its variant colors agree (Pelirroja, Negra).

## Plurals

CLDR categories: one / many / other (many = other, the counts are digits). The key without a suffix carries the « other » form (the « one » form
where the English bare key is singular: reward.decoration, reward.discovery_egg, reward.sticker), and reward.gems keeps
the « + » of the level reward card.
