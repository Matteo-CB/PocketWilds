# Pocket Wilds glossary: Simplified Chinese (zh-Hans)

Draft of 9 October 2026, agent TR1. Fixed terms for every Simplified Chinese text of the game. Market: Singapore,
Malaysia and Chinese speakers abroad (mainland China is not targeted, Localization Plan 1.2). The Traditional file
(`zh-Hant.md`) is written for Taiwan with its own words: never convert one into the other character by character.
Source: `Localization/Drafts/zh-Hans.json`. Forbidden words: `zh-Hans_forbidden.txt`. Questions: `Drafts/NOTES_TR1.md`.

## Tone

- Warm, gentle, playful; the player is 你. Soft particles where natural: 吧, 呢, 哦 (「梳梳毛吧」「上周的份为你留着呢」).
- System messages stay plain and kind: 「购买失败，请再试一次。」
- Goals are short verb phrases: 「孵化{n}颗蛋」「给{animal}梳毛」.
- Never pushy: no 限时, 赶快, 错过, 立即.
- Full-width punctuation ，。！？：（）; quotes “ ” around a name inside a sentence; ellipsis ……
- No space between Chinese characters and numbers: 「{n}只动物」. A space only before a symbol in short UI labels
  (「花色 +{pct}%」「连续 ×{n}」).
- Brand: Pocket Wilds stays in Latin letters.
