# Pocket Wilds, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan): forbidden words (owner rule of 2026-10-06, Docs/Design/Vocabulary_Rule.md).
# Nothing religious or divine in any text. This file and the rule document are the only places these words may appear.
# Format: one word or stem per line; the comment line above each entry gives its meaning.
# Chinese has no spaces between words: every entry is matched anywhere in a text (substring).
# Kept allowed on purpose: 星座 (constellations), 星光 (starlight), 睡蓮 (water lily), 燈籠 (everyday lantern),
# 水仙 and 仙人掌 (plants), 拜託 (please), 靈活 (nimble).
# Replacements: refuge = 家園, top rarity = 夢幻, glow = 之光 / 光點, rendez-vous = 每日相聚.
