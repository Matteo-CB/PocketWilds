# Pocket Wilds, Korean: forbidden words (owner rule of 2026-10-06, Docs/Design/Vocabulary_Rule.md).
# Nothing religious or divine in any text. This file and the rule document are the only places these words may appear.
# Format: one word or stem per line; the comment line above each entry gives its meaning.
# Matching (check_drafts.py): a plain entry matches at the start of a word (a stem: particles and endings may follow);
# "~" matches anywhere (inside compounds), "=" only the whole word. Korean particles attach to nouns, so stems are used.
# Avoided on purpose as too short or too common: 신 inside words (신나요, 신기록), 절 (절반), 선 (line), 오라 (come).
# Replacements: refuge = 보금자리, top rarity = 환상, glow = 햇살 / 빛, rendez-vous = 매일의 만남, water lily = 수련.
