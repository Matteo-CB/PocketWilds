# Pocket Wilds glossary: Traditional Chinese, Taiwan (zh-Hant)

Draft of 9 October 2026, agent TR1. Fixed terms for every Traditional Chinese text of the game, written for Taiwan
(and read in Hong Kong). Taiwan words, not a character conversion of the Simplified file: 影片 (not 视频), 公尺 (not
米), 機率, 票券, 躲貓貓, 咖啡色, 收成, 牠 for an animal, the particles 喔 and 囉.
Source: `Localization/Drafts/zh-Hant.json`. Forbidden words: `zh-Hant_forbidden.txt`. Questions: `Drafts/NOTES_TR1.md`.

## Tone

- Warm, gentle, playful; the player is 你; an animal is 牠. Soft Taiwan particles where natural: 吧, 喔, 囉
  (「票券在路上囉」「上週的份幫你留著喔」).
- System messages stay plain and kind: 「購買失敗，請再試一次。」
- Goals are short verb phrases, with 幫 for doing something for an animal: 「幫{animal}梳毛」.
- Never pushy: no 限時, 趕快, 錯過, 立即.
- Full-width punctuation ，。！？：（）; quotes 「」 around a name inside a sentence; ellipsis ……
- No space between Chinese characters and numbers: 「{n}隻動物」. A space only before a symbol in short UI labels.
- Brand: Pocket Wilds stays in Latin letters.
