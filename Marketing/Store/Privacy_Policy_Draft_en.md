## 4. Game statistics (only with your consent)

**What they are.** Short technical records of how the game is played, for example: a session started or ended, how long the game took to load, an egg was hatched (which egg, which animal and rarity), a screen was opened, an activity was played and its score, an offer was shown or touched, a purchase succeeded or failed, how smoothly the game ran (frame times, memory). Each batch also says which version of the game, which phone model, which operating system and how much memory the phone has.

**What they never contain.** Your name, the names you give to animals, anything you type, your contacts or your location. Statistics use a random statistics number created by the game. It is not linked to your profile number or to your phone's identifiers. **[LEGAL CHECK: whether this random number is personal data.]**

**Your choice.** The game asks you before any statistics leave your phone. **[CHECK: the consent screen is designed in the legal drafts but not built yet; it must ship before any statistics are sent.]**
- If you say yes, the statistics are sent to our statistics service.
- If you say no, the game stops recording statistics and deletes the ones waiting on your phone.
- Until you answer, nothing is sent.
- You can change your answer at any time in the game settings. **[CHECK: the settings screen is planned; it must exist before statistics are switched on.]**

**Why.** To understand what players enjoy, find what is confusing or too hard, fix slow screens, and balance the game.

## 5. Purchases

The game is free to play and offers optional in-app purchases. Payments are made through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive your card number, bank details or billing address.

To give you what you bought, and to restore it on a new device, the game receives a confirmation from the store (which product, when, and a purchase reference). **[OPEN DECISION: if purchases are checked on our server to prevent fraud, say what is sent and how long it is kept.]** If you have agreed to statistics, the result of a purchase (product, success or failure, reference price) is also part of the statistics.

You can turn off in-app purchases in your phone's settings (parental controls of Google Play and Apple).
