# 30-day content plan: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts

Written on 9 October 2026 for Pocket Wilds (working title). English only. One vertical video per day, made from the real game.

## Before you start

- **Format.** Vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Record the game in the editor or on a phone, then crop. The game's captures are 1080 × 2340: keep the action in the middle, leave room at the top and bottom for the app's buttons and captions.
- **Sound.** Use the game's own music and sounds. They are original, so there is no rights problem and they make the account recognizable. Avoid trending songs: on a TikTok Business account only the Commercial Music Library is cleared for brands.
- **Only real footage.** Every shot comes from the game or from its real production tools (Blender, the editor). No mock-ups of features that are not in the build. If a feature is planned, say "on our list", never a date.
- **Honesty lines.** Say "every animal is modeled for the game" and "original music and sounds". Never say "made without AI" or "handmade" (the code and the models were produced with AI help, even though no image, sound or voice generator was used). No "#1", no fake reviews, no download numbers.
- **Project rules.** No four-point star emoji or symbol in captions, hooks, thumbnails or hashtags (project rule in `Docs/Design/`); round five-point stars, hearts, paws and flowers are fine. Nothing religious, including in jokes and hashtags (`Docs/Design/Vocabulary_Rule.md`). Say "refuge" or "meadow".
- **Audience.** The posts speak to teens and adults who love cozy games, not to young children (this keeps the store's target-audience answer consistent). No "for kids" wording.

Legend for each day: **Concept**, **Shots** (from the real game), **Hook** (on-screen text in the first 1.5 s, 3 to 6 words), **Caption** (1 to 2 short lines), **Hashtags** (3 to 6), **Length**.

Mix over 30 days: 4 hatch reveals (days 1, 7, 19, 25), 5 refuge life including the daily visit (2, 9, 12, 17, 24), 6 ASMR (3, 8, 11, 15, 21, 27), 5 "can you spot them?" (4, 10, 16, 22, 29), 5 devlog (5, 13, 18, 23, 28), 4 community prompts (6, 14, 20, 26) and 1 recap (30).

## Posting tips

1. **Hook in 1.5 seconds.** The first frame already shows the action (an egg cracking, a nose behind a bush) and the hook text is on screen from frame one. No logo intro, no black frame.
2. **One idea per video.** One egg, one brush, one hiding spot. Short videos that people watch to the end and replay do better than long ones.
3. **Consistency beats perfection.** One post a day at about the same time. Batch-record on one day (10 clips), edit on another, schedule the week.
4. **When to post.** Start with a lunch-time or early-evening slot in the time zone of the main audience (English-speaking players: US and UK). After two weeks, check each app's analytics (when your followers are active) and move the slot.
5. **Reply to comments.** Answer the first comments within the first hour. Turn good questions into the next video (TikTok and Instagram let you reply to a comment with a video). Pin the best name suggestion.
6. **One video, three platforms.** Export a clean version without any app watermark and post it natively on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Keep captions and hashtags, adapt the first words to each platform. Do not upload a TikTok file with its watermark to the others.
7. **Captions on screen.** Many people watch muted: put the key words on screen, in large, high-contrast text away from the bottom edge (buttons and captions cover it).
8. **Loop.** End on a frame that matches the start (the egg before and after) so the video loops naturally.
9. **Series.** Repeat the formats that work: "Can you spot them?" every few days, "Name this..." every week, ASMR every weekend.
10. **Stay true to the build.** If something is planned and not in the game, it is a devlog with "on our list", never a gameplay clip.

## Brand voice guide (one page)

**Who we are.** A small team making a soft, cozy meadow where little animals hatch, play and feel safe. We talk like a friend showing you something cute on their phone.

**Our voice is:**
- **Warm.** We sound happy to see you. "Say hi to Mochi."
- **Gentle.** Calm words, no shouting, no pressure, no ALL CAPS.
- **Playful.** Small jokes, soft surprises. "Tiny crunches. That's it. That's the video."
- **Honest.** We show the real game. Odds are shown, nothing random is for sale, and we never pretend a feature exists before it does.

**We never:**
- push: no "Download NOW", no "Last chance", no countdowns, no FOMO;
- guilt: no "Your animals miss you", no "Don't break your streak" (there are no streaks);
- exaggerate: no "#1", "best game ever", fake reviews or numbers;
- talk down to anyone, or address young children directly;
- use the four-point star emoji or symbol, or any religious word or symbol (see the project rules);
- claim the game was made without AI.

**Words we like:** cozy, cute, soft, gentle, meadow, refuge, little, tiny, hatch, hop, nibble, brush, bow, peekaboo, wish, treat, friend, golden hour, calm.

**Emojis we use, sparingly (one or two per caption):** 🐰 🐿️ 🦔 🥚 🐾 🌼 🌈 🍀 💛 ⭐ (round five-point star only).

**How a caption sounds:**

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| OMG you NEED this game!!! | A little meadow for your quiet moments. |
| Don't miss out, pre-register now! | Pre-registration is open, link in bio if you'd like to join us. |
| The most addictive cozy game ever | We hope this makes your day a bit softer. |
| Come back daily or lose your rewards! | A little gift waits every day. No streaks, no pressure. |

**Replying to comments.** Thank people by name when you can, answer questions simply, never argue. If someone criticizes, say thank you and what you will look at. If a question is about money or data, answer with the facts from the store page (optional purchases, odds shown, nothing random for sale).
